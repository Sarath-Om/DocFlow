1. Summary

DocFlow does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your documents stay on your device. You decide when to export or share files.

2. Who we are

DocFlow is provided by TODO: DEVELOPER_NAME. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

3. Information we do not collect

DocFlow does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name or email, analytics, advertising identifiers, location, crash reports, or document contents. We do not receive copies of your documents.

4. Information stored on your device

Your documents, scans, OCR text, folders, tags, settings and Private Safe contents are stored in the app's private storage or in file locations you choose. The developer cannot access these files through DocFlow.

5. Permissions and why

  • Camera: used to scan documents when you choose to scan.
  • Storage or folder access: Android's system picker lets you open files and folders you select, such as Downloads.
  • Biometrics: optional unlocking for Private Safe or app lock, if enabled.
  • Notifications: requested only if a feature needs them.

Permissions are optional for the related feature and can be revoked in Android system settings.

6. Network use

DocFlow works offline. The optional download of on-device translation language packs may contact Google's ML Kit model servers through Google libraries. No document content is sent as part of that download. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to those services. The APK is hosted on GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.

7. On-device processing

OCR, summarising and translation are designed to run on your device. Availability depends on the selected feature and any required language model.

8. Encryption and security

Optional file encryption and Private Safe use AES-256 with keys held in the Android Keystore. If you forget your PIN or lose your device, we cannot recover encrypted files for you. No software or storage method is perfectly secure.

9. Backups and sharing

Any export, backup (including .docflow files) or share is started by you and goes to the location or recipient you choose. Their privacy practices then apply.

10. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it, move items to Recently deleted (which are automatically purged after 30 days), clear app storage, or uninstall DocFlow. The developer holds no copy.

11. Children's privacy

DocFlow is not directed at children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

12. Your rights

Because the developer does not hold personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. You control data stored in the app. We aim to handle privacy consistently with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws to the extent they apply; this statement is not a claim that every provision applies in every case.

13. Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
