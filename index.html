GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is DocFlow free?

Yes. DocFlow is free to download and use.

Does it need an internet connection?

Core document browsing and organization work offline. Downloading the app and optional language models requires internet.

Where are my files stored?

Files you open or create are stored on your device or in the location you choose. DocFlow's private data stays in its app storage.

Does DocFlow upload my documents?

No. DocFlow does not upload your documents to us. Exporting or sharing happens only when you choose it.

Which formats are supported?

DocFlow supports PDF and common Office document formats, including DOCX, PPTX and XLSX. Exact support can vary by feature.

How do I delete my data?

Delete files in the app, clear DocFlow's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Back up files you want to keep first.
