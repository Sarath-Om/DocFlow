3. Information we do NOT collect

When you use DocFlow in offline mode, we do not collect, log, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • No mandatory accounts: You can open, scan, edit, annotate, and export documents offline without creating an account.
  • No names, phone numbers, or contact lists: We do not read your address book or profile details.
  • No analytics or telemetry: Neither the Android app nor this website includes Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, cookies, or behavioral tracking scripts.
  • No advertising IDs: DocFlow contains zero advertisements and never requests the Android Advertising ID (AAID).
  • No third-party crash reporting: We do not bundle third-party crash-reporting SDKs that silently upload stack traces or device identifiers.
  • No location data: DocFlow never requests coarse or fine GPS/network location permissions.
  • No document contents sent to us: Your PDFs, Office files, scans, OCR text, versions, and notes are processed locally on your device.

4. Information stored on your device only

Your documents, scans, ML Kit OCR text, version history snapshots, notes/comments, PDF ink annotations, folders, custom tags, app settings, and Private Safe contents live inside the app's private storage directory (files/docflow_documents/, files/scanned_pdfs/, files/annotated_pdfs/) and local Room SQLite database (docflow_local_cache.db with FTS4 full-text search index) on your phone.

Android isolates this private storage so other apps cannot read it, and we have no technical means to access files stored on your device.

5. Permissions and why

DocFlow follows the principle of least privilege and requests only the permissions required for the features you actively use:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used only when you open the document scanner to capture physical pages with CameraX.
  • Storage / folder access via the system picker: DocFlow uses Android's built-in system file picker (Storage Access Framework) and VIEW / SEND intents so the app only accesses the specific files or folders (such as Downloads) that you explicitly choose.
  • Biometrics (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC): Optional. Used via Android's system BiometricPrompt to unlock Private Safe documents, the app entry lock, and biometric document signatures.
  • Notifications: Requested only if a specific feature needs to show status notifications.

Each permission is optional and can be revoked at any time in your phone's system settings.

6. Network use

DocFlow works offline. In offline mode, the only network activity is the optional one-time download of on-device recognition or translation language packs, fetched directly from Google's ML Kit model servers by the Google ML Kit libraries included in the app. No document content is ever sent during model downloads. Google's own terms of service and privacy policy apply to those model downloads.

When you download the DocFlow APK file from GitHub Releases, that download is served by GitHub, Inc., which operates under GitHub's own privacy policy.

7. On-device processing

All document processing—including CameraX edge detection, Google ML Kit Optical Character Recognition (OCR), full-text FTS4 indexing, summarising, translation, Office-to-PDF conversion, and exporting individual document versions or notes to PDF—runs locally on your device's processor.

8. Encryption and security

Optional file encryption and Private Safe protection use AES-256 with keys protected via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and the Android Keystore / androidx.biometric.

Important warning: If you forget your PIN/passphrase or lose your device, we cannot recover encrypted files, and no software is perfectly secure.

9. Backups and sharing

Any export (annotated PDF, version snapshot PDF, note PDF, or PNG), backup (.docflow), or share is started by you and goes only where you send it.

10. Data retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it, move items to Recently deleted (auto-purged after 30 days), clear app storage, or uninstall the app. We hold no copy.

11. Children's privacy

DocFlow is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country); we collect no data from anyone.

12. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you in offline mode, there is nothing to access or delete on our side; you control everything in the app. This design aligns with the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws to the extent they apply.
