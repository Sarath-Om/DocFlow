Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about pricing, offline operation, storage, and data ownership.

Is it free? +
Yes. DocFlow is completely free to download and use, with no subscriptions, no locked paywalls, and no advertisements.
Does it need internet? +
No. Viewing, editing, CameraX document scanning, ML Kit OCR text extraction, PDF markup, version snapshots, and exporting documents/versions/notes to PDF all work 100% offline. Internet is only used if you download an optional on-device translation language pack from Google ML Kit model servers or explicitly enable workspace sync.
Where are my files stored? +
Your imported documents, scanned PDFs, version snapshots, notes, and Private Safe files are stored locally inside DocFlow's private application directory and Room SQLite database on your phone. Other apps cannot read your private app storage unless you explicitly export or share a file.
Does DocFlow upload my documents? +
In local offline mode, DocFlow never uploads your documents anywhere. All OCR, summarising, translation, and PDF generation run on your device. Any file export or share happens only when you tap Share or Export.
Which formats are supported? +
DocFlow supports PDF (.pdf), Microsoft Word (.doc, .docx), Microsoft Excel (.xls, .xlsx, .csv), Microsoft PowerPoint (.ppt, .pptx), plain text (.txt), and images (.png, .jpg, .webp). You can also convert documents to high-resolution PNGs or export any document version or note as a PDF file.
How do I delete my data? +
You can delete individual documents, folders, annotations, or version snapshots directly inside the app. To permanently erase all data at once, uninstall the DocFlow app or open Android Settings → Apps → DocFlow → Storage & cache → Clear storage.
