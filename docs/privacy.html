3. Information we do NOT collect

When you use DocFlow in offline mode, we do not collect, log, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • No mandatory accounts: You can open, scan, edit, annotate, and export documents offline without creating an account.
  • No names, phone numbers, or contact lists: We do not read your address book or profile details.
  • No analytics or telemetry: Neither the Android app nor this website includes Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, cookies, or behavioral tracking scripts.
  • No advertising IDs: DocFlow contains zero advertisements and never requests the Android Advertising ID (AAID).
  • No third-party crash reporting: We do not bundle third-party crash-reporting SDKs that silently upload stack traces or device identifiers.
  • No location data: DocFlow never requests coarse or fine GPS/network location permissions.
  • No document contents sent to us: Your PDFs, Office files, scans, OCR text, versions, and notes are processed locally on your device.

4. Information stored on your device only

Your documents, scans, ML Kit OCR text, version history snapshots, notes/comments, PDF ink annotations, folders, custom tags, app settings, and Private Safe contents live inside the app's private storage directory (files/docflow_documents/, files/scanned_pdfs/, files/annotated_pdfs/) and local Room SQLite database (docflow_local_cache.db with FTS4 full-text search index) on your phone.

Android isolates this private storage so other apps cannot read it, and we have no technical means to access files stored on your device. If you optionally sign in and enable workspace cloud sync, your selected workspace records are synchronized with your own authenticated cloud database; you can enable Offline-First Room FTS4 Mode at any time so all activity stays strictly local.

5. Permissions and why

DocFlow follows the principle of least privilege and requests only the permissions required for the features you actively use:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used only when you open the document scanner to capture physical pages with CameraX. Marked as non-required hardware so DocFlow installs on devices without a camera.
  • Storage / folder access via the system picker: DocFlow does not request broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). Instead, it uses Android's built-in system file picker (Storage Access Framework) and VIEW / SEND intents so the app only accesses the specific files or folders (such as Downloads) that you explicitly choose.
  • Biometrics (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC): Optional. Used via Android's system BiometricPrompt to unlock Private Safe documents, the app entry lock, and biometric document signatures. Your fingerprint or face data is handled strictly by the Android operating system and is never exposed to DocFlow.
  • Notifications: Requested only if a specific feature needs to show status notifications.

Each permission is optional and can be revoked at any time in your phone's Android Settings → Apps → DocFlow → Permissions.

6. Network use

DocFlow works offline. In offline mode, the only network activity is the optional one-time download of on-device recognition or translation language packs, fetched directly from Google's ML Kit model servers by the Google ML Kit libraries included in the app. No document content is ever sent during model downloads. Google's own terms of service and privacy policy apply to those model downloads.

When you download the DocFlow APK file from GitHub Releases, that download is served by GitHub, Inc., which operates under GitHub's own privacy policy.

7. On-device processing

All document processing—including CameraX edge detection, Google ML Kit Optical Character Recognition (OCR), full-text FTS4 indexing, summarising, translation, Office-to-PDF conversion, and exporting individual document versions or notes to PDF—runs locally on your device's processor.

8. Encryption and security

Optional file encryption and Private Safe protection use AES-256 (with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derivation and random cryptographic salts/initialization vectors) alongside hardware-backed authentication through the Android Keystore and androidx.biometric.

Important warning: Because we never store or transmit your PIN, passphrase, or encryption keys, if you forget your PIN/passphrase or lose your device, we cannot recover your encrypted files. While we use industry-standard cryptography, no software or device can guarantee absolute security.

9. Backups and sharing

Automatic OS cloud backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false") to prevent silent background uploads of your private documents. Any export (such as exporting an annotated PDF, exporting a specific document version snapshot or note as a PDF, or exporting PNG images), backup (.docflow), or share via Android's system share sheet is started explicitly by you and goes only to the destination app or folder you choose.

10. Data retention and deletion

Your files, OCR indexes, version history, and notes remain on your device until you delete them inside the app, move items to Recently deleted (auto-purged after 30 days), clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall DocFlow. We hold no copy of your local files.

11. Children's privacy

DocFlow is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children or anyone else.

12. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on our servers when you use DocFlow offline, there is no personal profile for us to access, correct, export, or delete on our side—you exercise full control directly inside the app on your device.

This local-first architecture is designed to respect your privacy principles under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and similar data protection laws to the extent they apply. If you contact us by email, you may request deletion of your email correspondence at any time.
